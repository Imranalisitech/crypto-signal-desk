TP1/TP2/TP3 are 1R/2R/3R from the proposed stop distance. The stop uses recent swing structure and ATR padding, with a maximum-distance guard. Leverage does not reduce the loss if the stop is missed. Fees, funding payments, slippage, liquidation price, maintenance margin, tick/lot-size filters and partial fills are not included. Do not treat the displayed quantity as an exchange-ready order size.
Institutional-data boundary: this public-data dashboard cannot identify actual institutions or prove their trades. Top-trader ratios are exchange-defined cohort aggregates, not audited institutional positions. Actual liquidation prints/heatmaps, true multi-venue CVD, options gamma, ETF flows, on-chain whale wallet attribution and prime-broker positioning require specialist data feeds and are not fabricated here. The squeeze-risk field is a clearly labelled proxy only. Taker buy/sell is estimated from recent Binance trades; it is not full-market CVD.
