Automatic SL / TP: The setup supplies the initial structural SL and a final 3R TP. For the profit-lock simulation: at 50% of TP distance protect 5% of the total entry-to-TP price range; at 75% of TP distance protect 50% of that range. This applies equally to LONG and SHORT. These are PRICE levels, not guaranteed realized returns. A moved Binance stop requires working connection, confirmed active position, Binance order acceptance and explicit live activation. Existing protection stays in place if the replacement fails.

Settings are not overwritten while editing. The daily loss limit must be enforced and audited by the live execution engine before automatic trading can be enabled.
